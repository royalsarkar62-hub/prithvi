About PRITHVI 🌿

PRITHVI is an educational plant-identification project designed to make botanical exploration simple and accessible. It uses the Pl@ntNet API for image-based identification.

Identification results should always be treated as AI-assisted estimates and not as professional botanical, medical or food-safety advice.
